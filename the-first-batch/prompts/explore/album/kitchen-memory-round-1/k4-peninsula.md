THE MEMORY · shared by all nine frames

It is 1995 in a lower middle class American suburban starter house. The family of four moved in this year. The kitchen is a leftover from the 1970s because there was never money to renovate. This photograph is the kitchen the way a grown child remembers it from 2026: glowing, perfect, too clean. Every surface is spotless, glossy and waxed, every object placed just so, nothing worn, nothing chipped, nothing out of place. It is lived in, 1995, not a 1970s showroom, but immaculate, like a memory polished a little more on every visit.

SIZE · the most important thing. This is a small, modest, builder grade kitchen with a standard 8 foot ceiling, sized exactly as stated in the frame. Not a luxury kitchen, not a big open plan, not a renovated home. The wide angle lens shows the whole room corner to corner, the ceiling and the floor both in frame, so the true small size reads. Rectilinear wide angle lens, vertical lines kept straight, no fisheye.

ALWAYS · 70s ceramic tile in an orange family somewhere in the room. No wallpaper. Painted walls. No people, no hands, no pets. No logos, no brand names, no readable text: any mail, calendar, cereal box or label stays blank or blurred. No dough. Square 1:1 frame composed like an album cover with one clear focal point.

FRAME 4 · THE PENINSULA FROM THE DINING ROOM

Room: a modest peninsula kitchen, about 12 by 13 feet. A wood topped breakfast bar peninsula separates the kitchen from the dining area.
Camera: standing back in the dining area, lens about 4 feet off the floor, looking across the peninsula into the kitchen, the edge of the dining table and one dining chair in the near foreground. 16mm full frame equivalent, slight two point perspective.
Light: golden hour, low warm sun raking through the kitchen window across the peninsula, long soft shadows, honey glow on the wood.
Cabinets: teak slab doors with thin brushed aluminum pulls.
Tile: burnt orange square tile backsplash.
Appliances: avocado green refrigerator and range, a hood above the range.
Peninsula: wood top, teak sides, three chrome bar stools with orange vinyl seats tucked in neatly.
Floor: orange brown square floor tile.
Walls: warm cream.
Decor: a fruit bowl of plastic fruit on the bar, a beige wall phone with a coiled cord, a small TV with a VCR on the counter, its screen dark, kids' drawings on the fridge, a single tray of golden cookies on the peninsula as the focal point.
Window: a sunny green yard and a white picket fence, too perfect.
Another room: we stand in it. The dining area is part of the frame.
Photograph: medium format color negative film, warm, fine grain.
