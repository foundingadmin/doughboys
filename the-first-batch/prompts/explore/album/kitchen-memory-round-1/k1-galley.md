THE MEMORY · shared by all nine frames

It is 1995 in a lower middle class American suburban starter house. The family of four moved in this year. The kitchen is a leftover from the 1970s because there was never money to renovate. This photograph is the kitchen the way a grown child remembers it from 2026: glowing, perfect, too clean. Every surface is spotless, glossy and waxed, every object placed just so, nothing worn, nothing chipped, nothing out of place. It is lived in, 1995, not a 1970s showroom, but immaculate, like a memory polished a little more on every visit.

SIZE · the most important thing. This is a small, modest, builder grade kitchen with a standard 8 foot ceiling, sized exactly as stated in the frame. Not a luxury kitchen, not a big open plan, not a renovated home. The wide angle lens shows the whole room corner to corner, the ceiling and the floor both in frame, so the true small size reads. Rectilinear wide angle lens, vertical lines kept straight, no fisheye.

ALWAYS · 70s ceramic tile in an orange family somewhere in the room. No wallpaper. Painted walls. No people, no hands, no pets. No logos, no brand names, no readable text: any mail, calendar, cereal box or label stays blank or blurred. No dough. Square 1:1 frame composed like an album cover with one clear focal point.

FRAME 1 · THE GALLEY

Room: a tiny galley kitchen, about 8 by 11 feet, two facing runs of cabinets with a narrow aisle of floor between them and a single window at the far end over the sink.
Camera: standing in the doorway at the open end, lens about 4 feet off the floor at a ten year old's eye height, squared up, one point perspective straight down the aisle to the window. 16mm full frame equivalent.
Light: late afternoon, golden sun pouring straight down the galley from the far window, a long warm stripe across the floor, soft haze glow.
Cabinets: dark walnut slab doors, no hardware, finger pulls, satin polish.
Tile: burnt orange 4 inch square tile on the counters and the backsplash, cream grout.
Appliances: harvest gold range on the left run, harvest gold refrigerator on the right run.
Floor: glossy brown and gold patterned sheet vinyl.
Walls and ceiling: warm cream paint, a round frosted ceiling fixture.
Seating: none, just a small yellow plastic step stool.
Decor, all spotless: a ceramic rooster on the counter, a bowl of plastic fruit, matching orange dish towels on the oven handle, a teal and grape Tupperware stack, a beige wall phone with a long coiled cord.
Window: a perfect green yard and a white picket fence, so perfect it looks painted on.
Another room: none, the galley is closed.
Photograph: medium format color negative film, warm, fine grain, soft halation on the highlights.
