THE MEMORY · shared by all nine frames

It is 1995 in a lower middle class American suburban starter house. The family of four moved in this year. The kitchen is a leftover from the 1970s because there was never money to renovate. This photograph is the kitchen the way a grown child remembers it from 2026: glowing, perfect, too clean. Every surface is spotless, glossy and waxed, every object placed just so, nothing worn, nothing chipped, nothing out of place. It is lived in, 1995, not a 1970s showroom, but immaculate, like a memory polished a little more on every visit.

SIZE · the most important thing. This is a small, modest, builder grade kitchen with a standard 8 foot ceiling, sized exactly as stated in the frame. Not a luxury kitchen, not a big open plan, not a renovated home. The wide angle lens shows the whole room corner to corner, the ceiling and the floor both in frame, so the true small size reads. Rectilinear wide angle lens, vertical lines kept straight, no fisheye.

ALWAYS · 70s ceramic tile in an orange family somewhere in the room. No wallpaper. Painted walls. No people, no hands, no pets. No logos, no brand names, no readable text: any mail, calendar, cereal box or label stays blank or blurred. No dough. Square 1:1 frame composed like an album cover with one clear focal point.

FRAME 5 · THE ONE WALL KITCHEN AT DUSK

Room: a tiny one wall kitchen, a single 10 foot run of cabinets and appliances against the back wall of a small open living and dining room about 13 by 20 feet total. A short counter with an overhang juts out at one end.
Camera: from the living room side, lens about 4 feet off the floor, the back of a plaid sofa and a strip of shag carpet in the near foreground, the whole kitchen wall across the room. 14mm full frame equivalent, straight on.
Light: dusk, deep blue sky in the window, the room lit warm by table lamps, the glowing range hood light and a single pendant over the short counter. Cozy amber pools of light.
Cabinets: white laminate slab doors with walnut edge trim and long walnut pulls.
Tile: chocolate brown and orange tile backsplash in a simple stripe.
Appliances: chocolate brown refrigerator and chocolate brown range.
Floor: cream linoleum in the kitchen meeting brown shag carpet in the living room.
Walls: warm beige.
Seating: two rattan bar stools at the short counter.
Decor: a boom box on the counter, a cookie jar, a Tupperware tower in teal, grape and lime, a cordless phone, a bowl of plastic fruit, a tray of golden cookies under the pendant.
Window: blue hour sky over a neat yard and white picket fence.
Another room: the living room fills the foreground. The kitchen is seen across it.
Photograph: medium format color negative film, warm tungsten glow, soft grain.
