THE MEMORY · shared by all nine frames

It is 1995 in a lower middle class American suburban starter house. The family of four moved in this year. The kitchen is a leftover from the 1970s because there was never money to renovate. This photograph is the kitchen the way a grown child remembers it from 2026: glowing, perfect, too clean. Every surface is spotless, glossy and waxed, every object placed just so, nothing worn, nothing chipped, nothing out of place. It is lived in, 1995, not a 1970s showroom, but immaculate, like a memory polished a little more on every visit.

SIZE · the most important thing. This is a small, modest, builder grade kitchen with a standard 8 foot ceiling, sized exactly as stated in the frame. Not a luxury kitchen, not a big open plan, not a renovated home. The wide angle lens shows the whole room corner to corner, the ceiling and the floor both in frame, so the true small size reads. Rectilinear wide angle lens, vertical lines kept straight, no fisheye.

ALWAYS · 70s ceramic tile in an orange family somewhere in the room. No wallpaper. Painted walls. No people, no hands, no pets. No logos, no brand names, no readable text: any mail, calendar, cereal box or label stays blank or blurred. No dough. Square 1:1 frame composed like an album cover with one clear focal point.

FRAME 2 · THE L WITH A TABLE

Room: a small L shaped eat in kitchen, about 11 by 12 feet. Cabinets run along two walls, a small round table fills the open corner.
Camera: in the opposite corner, lens about 4 feet off the floor, two point perspective so both cabinet walls recede, 14mm full frame equivalent, the table close in the foreground.
Light: early morning, low sun through a window over the sink throwing long sunbeams across the table and floor, gentle haze in the air.
Cabinets: honey oak with cathedral arch raised panels and brass knobs.
Tile: small glossy orange mosaic tile backsplash. Cream laminate counters with oak edge.
Appliances: avocado green refrigerator and avocado range.
Floor: sunny yellow brick pattern linoleum, freshly waxed.
Walls: pale butter yellow paint.
Seating: a round maple table with four spindle back chairs, four matching placemats, a jug of orange juice.
Decor: a cookie jar, a fridge covered neatly in magnets and kids' crayon drawings, a cordless phone in its cradle, a lime green Tupperware canister set, a sheet of golden cookies cooling on the counter.
Window: blue sky, a white picket fence, a tidy yard.
Another room: through a wide arched opening we see part of a wood paneled family room with a console television.
Photograph: medium format color negative film, warm, soft, gentle bloom.
