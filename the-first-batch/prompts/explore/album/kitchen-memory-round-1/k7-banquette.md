THE MEMORY · shared by all nine frames

It is 1995 in a lower middle class American suburban starter house. The family of four moved in this year. The kitchen is a leftover from the 1970s because there was never money to renovate. This photograph is the kitchen the way a grown child remembers it from 2026: glowing, perfect, too clean. Every surface is spotless, glossy and waxed, every object placed just so, nothing worn, nothing chipped, nothing out of place. It is lived in, 1995, not a 1970s showroom, but immaculate, like a memory polished a little more on every visit.

SIZE · the most important thing. This is a small, modest, builder grade kitchen with a standard 8 foot ceiling, sized exactly as stated in the frame. Not a luxury kitchen, not a big open plan, not a renovated home. The wide angle lens shows the whole room corner to corner, the ceiling and the floor both in frame, so the true small size reads. Rectilinear wide angle lens, vertical lines kept straight, no fisheye.

ALWAYS · 70s ceramic tile in an orange family somewhere in the room. No wallpaper. Painted walls. No people, no hands, no pets. No logos, no brand names, no readable text: any mail, calendar, cereal box or label stays blank or blurred. No dough. Square 1:1 frame composed like an album cover with one clear focal point.

FRAME 7 · THE BIG EAT IN WITH A BANQUETTE

Room: the largest kitchen a modest house gets, an eat in kitchen about 14 by 15 feet, still plain and builder grade. Cabinets on two walls, a built in breakfast nook banquette in one corner, a sliding glass door to the back yard.
Camera: low in the banquette corner, lens about 3.5 feet off the floor, the banquette table edge in the near foreground, looking across the whole room. 12mm full frame equivalent, very wide but rectilinear.
Light: late afternoon sun through cafe curtains and the sliding glass door, long warm rectangles of light across the floor and the table, warm haze.
Cabinets: knotty pine with black wrought iron hinges.
Tile: orange tile backsplash with a few hand painted accent tiles.
Appliances: coppertone range and coppertone refrigerator.
Floor: red brown brick pattern vinyl.
Walls: warm white.
Seating: an orange vinyl banquette and a laminate table with chrome edging.
Decor: a ceramic rooster, a bowl of plastic fruit, a cookie jar, a calendar with a blank page, a lime and grape Tupperware stack, a beige wall phone, a tray of golden cookies in the middle of the banquette table.
Window and door: through the sliding glass door, a perfect green yard and a white picket fence like a painted backdrop.
Another room: the back yard through the glass. No other interior room.
Photograph: medium format color negative film, warm, soft, nostalgic.
