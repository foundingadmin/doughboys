THE MEMORY · shared by all nine frames

It is 1995 in a lower middle class American suburban starter house. The family of four moved in this year. The kitchen is a leftover from the 1970s because there was never money to renovate. This photograph is the kitchen the way a grown child remembers it from 2026: glowing, perfect, too clean. Every surface is spotless, glossy and waxed, every object placed just so, nothing worn, nothing chipped, nothing out of place. It is lived in, 1995, not a 1970s showroom, but immaculate, like a memory polished a little more on every visit.

SIZE · the most important thing. This is a small, modest, builder grade kitchen with a standard 8 foot ceiling, sized exactly as stated in the frame. Not a luxury kitchen, not a big open plan, not a renovated home. The wide angle lens shows the whole room corner to corner, the ceiling and the floor both in frame, so the true small size reads. Rectilinear wide angle lens, vertical lines kept straight, no fisheye.

ALWAYS · 70s ceramic tile in an orange family somewhere in the room. No wallpaper. Painted walls. No people, no hands, no pets. No logos, no brand names, no readable text: any mail, calendar, cereal box or label stays blank or blurred. No dough. Square 1:1 frame composed like an album cover with one clear focal point.

FRAME 8 · THE FAMILY SNAPSHOT

Room: a small L shaped kitchen, about 10 by 10 feet, with a small drop leaf table against one wall and a doorway into a hallway.
Camera: a 1995 family snapshot taken from the doorway by someone standing, slightly off level, a compact point and shoot zoom camera set to its widest 28mm. Still shows the whole room, ceiling and floor.
Light: evening, the on camera flash firing, bright flat front light and a hard shadow behind objects, the warm frosted ceiling dome light glowing, a dark window.
Cabinets: dark brown wood grain laminate with almond trim.
Tile: orange tile backsplash.
Appliances: avocado green range and a newer almond refrigerator that does not match.
Floor: harvest gold geometric sheet vinyl.
Walls: almond paint.
Seating: a small drop leaf table with two chairs.
Decor: a birthday cake on the table with unlit candles, plates stacked neatly, a fridge covered in magnets and kids' drawings, a cordless phone, a teal Tupperware set, a TV with a VCR on the counter.
Window: night, black glass reflecting the flash.
Another room: through the doorway, part of a hallway with framed family photos on the wall, faces too small to see.
Photograph: Kodak Gold 200 color print film, saturated warm color, slight vignette, mild grain, the look of a drugstore photo print. No date stamp.
