A real 1990 mail order catalog photograph shot on medium format film with an 80mm lens, fine film grain, muted print color. Shot straight down from directly overhead, the camera square to the surface, a flat top down view in a square frame.

The whole frame is a smooth slab of raw bread dough, edge to edge, no rim, table or background showing. The frame covers about 30 centimeters of dough.

Satin matte skin with fine stretch lines and soft folds. Never dry or stringy: no strands, threads or torn edges stretching between pieces, no holes, no lace, no webbing. No pool, no puddle, nothing runny, wet, slimy or glossy. Shaped only by gravity, an object and never a creature.

Low soft raking light from the upper left, from a source well outside the frame, so the impression reads clearly in shadow and highlight.
