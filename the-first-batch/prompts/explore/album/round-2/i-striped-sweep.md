A real 1990 mail order catalog photograph shot on medium format film with an 80mm lens, fine film grain, muted print color. Shot straight down from directly overhead, the camera square to the surface, a flat top down view in a square frame.

The surface is nine vertical bands of seamless matte paper of equal width side by side, filling the frame edge to edge with no gaps, left to right in this exact order: blueberry blue, butter yellow, cherry red, bondi blue, bubblegum pink, lime green, grape purple, tangerine orange, mint green. Flat, evenly lit, crisp straight edges between the bands.

One big plump wad of raw bread dough, about the size of a loaf, lies across the middle of the frame, slumped low and wide across the middle five bands, a little left of center. One lazy fold on top, never a perfect round ball, never symmetrical.

Firm and moist, freshly mixed raw bread dough, thick, heavy, plump and saggy, one cohesive mass in one even pale oatmeal beige like raw pizza dough, never pink and never meaty, never tinted by the colors around it. Satin matte skin with fine stretch lines and soft folds. Never dry or stringy: no strands, threads or torn edges, no holes, no lace, no webbing. Nothing runny, wet, slimy or glossy. Shaped only by gravity, an object and never a creature.

Soft diffused light from the upper left, from a source well outside the frame, a soft contact shadow under the dough.

No people, no hands, no logos, no brand names, no readable text. No accidental faces: no pair of dimples, bubbles or holes that read as eyes.
