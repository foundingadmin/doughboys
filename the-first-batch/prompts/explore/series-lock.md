# Explore prompt · series lock

Every explore prompt is these blocks word for word, with only the bracketed parts changed per track. Set by Josh on Spice round 1, Oct 18 2026, refined through Oct 19 2026. Write the finished prompt to `work/` and pass it with `--prompt-file`.

## Template

```
A real 1990 mail order catalog photograph shot on medium format film with an 80mm lens, fine film grain, muted print color. [DEVICE, named part by part, with its analog knobs and buttons and a small glowing red power light where a real one would sit.] [REAL PROPORTIONS, for example: the body is only slightly larger than a standard audio cassette and the door is the full size of one.]

[DOUGH: the firm block below, plus this track's own silhouette from tracks.json, for example a brick shaped like a VHS tape half in the slot.]

Camera straight on at the device's own height, squared up to its front. The device fills about half the frame height. Every object, accessories and dough included, sits inside the frame with clear backdrop at every edge. Nothing cropped.

Seamless [GROUND NAME] paper sweep, no horizon line, no seams, no studio equipment. Soft diffused light from the upper left, from a source well outside the frame, soft contact shadow only. A brighter glow behind the device and on the floor, falling to a deeper [GROUND NAME] in the top corners.

No people, no hands, no logos, no brand names, no readable text.
```

## Dough blocks

**Firm, the default from Oct 19 2026**

```
Firm, freshly kneaded raw bread dough that holds its shape, one cohesive mass, pale oatmeal beige like raw pizza dough, never pink and never meaty. Satin matte skin with fine stretch lines and soft folds, no holes, no lace, no webbing. Where it meets the floor it stays thick and rounded. No pool, no puddle, nothing runny, wet or glossy. Shaped only by gravity.
```

**Poured, used on Deluge, Spice and Bopkins**

```
One cohesive mass of raw kneaded bread dough, pale oatmeal beige like raw pizza dough, never pink and never meaty. Satin matte skin with fine stretch lines, no holes, no lace, no webbing. One thick heavy lobe. The pool is a thick soft edged puddle with a dull satin surface, not liquid and not glossy. Shaped only by gravity.
```

## Rules that came from failures

- Never name a softbox. It put the light in frame on four of six Determinator frames. Describing the light without the word fixed all four (Oct 19 2026).
- A device that must show its top, like a turntable platter, gets a front view raised about 25 degrees, still squared up to the front.
- Never ask for a pink cast or gluten strands. Those words turned the dough into lace and raw meat on Spice pass A.
- Every track gets its own dough silhouette (a brick, a ball, a heap, a braid). Spice through Traycee all ended in a lobe sagging out the left, and the covers started to rhyme (Josh, Oct 19 2026).
- The dough's weight sits at the left third where it can, so the covers read as a set. That sets where the weight goes, never the shape.
- Dough is lifeless. It never rises, reaches, crawls, pushes a door open or gets ejected. No accidental faces or matched pairs that read as eyes.
- Exploration says no readable text. The 4K prompt supplies the exact label words later.
